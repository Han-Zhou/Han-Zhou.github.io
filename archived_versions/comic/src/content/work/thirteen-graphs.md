## Why thirteen?

Because three would fit too comfortably. This entry tests whether a long title can remain loud without colliding with its frame, and whether a short project can still look intentional inside the same template as a long technical report.

Possible sources of misreading include truncated axes, inconsistent scales, missing baselines, accidental area comparisons, and annotations that explain the designer’s intention rather than the data.
